Sluice

One client sign-off pays the whole subcontract chain on time, and no firm sees anyone else’s contract.

Built on Canton with Daml · working MVP on a local network, one node per firm

Sluice is for Indian IT subcontracting chains. When the end client accepts the work, every firm down the chain knows at once, its own legal payment clock starts, and no firm ever receives a contract it isn’t party to.
