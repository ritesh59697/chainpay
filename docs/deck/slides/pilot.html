PILOT PLAN

Three steps from one chain to MainNet

STEP 1 · DEVNET

One prime, two subcontractors

Real milestones from one live project; banks still move the money. Exit: every acceptance reaches the freelancer within a day.

STEP 2 · PROOF

Bank reference and Form I

Each receipt points to a real transfer; the half-year Form I list comes from Sluice. Exit: it matches the books.

STEP 3 · MAINNET

Production and growth

Move to MainNet, invite the rest of the chain, apply for Featured App status. Exit: the prime pays to continue.

Integrations by step: CSV export to accounting; bank statement import and a Form I report; then the Global Synchronizer on MainNet.
