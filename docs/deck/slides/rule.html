THE LAW SAYS THEY SHOULDN’T WAIT

Three dates every buyer of a small firm’s work must hit

45 days

Agreed payment terms to a registered micro or small supplier can’t exceed 45 days from accepting its work.

MSMED Act 2006, s.15

Day 46

Paying late adds compound interest, with monthly rests, at three times the RBI bank rate.

MSMED Act 2006, s.16

31 March

A sum still overdue and unpaid at year end isn’t tax-deductible that year, only when it’s paid.

Income-tax Act 2025, s.37(2)(g), formerly 43B(h)

Applies to suppliers registered (Udyam) as micro or small enterprises. Not tax or legal advice.

The 45 days come from the MSMED Act. The tax rule was section 43B(h) of the 1961 Act; a Rajya Sabha reply of 21 July 2026 confirms it continues as section 37(2)(g) of the Income-tax Act 2025. Companies also report dues over 45 days to small suppliers every half year on MSME Form I.
