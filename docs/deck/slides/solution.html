CHAINPAY

The acceptance flows down the chain. The contracts never do.

01

Each link is its own contract

Signed by its two firms only, on Canton. The ledger rejects terms over the legal cap.

02

One sign-off cascades

When the client accepts, each firm accepts its own supplier’s work at once. Each tier’s own 45-day clock starts, with its own due date.

03

Pay, with proof

Each payer pays once it’s paid and gets a receipt marked on time or late. Each firm keeps its own history for its accountant.

Back-to-back acceptance passes only the upstream milestone id and time as plain values. It never fetches the upstream contract, so Canton never sends that contract down the chain.
