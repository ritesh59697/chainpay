WHY IT ISN’T SOLVED

A shared portal would fix the information gap. It would also show every tier’s margin. So nobody joins one.

AcmeIT’s contract with the retailer shows what the retailer pays. Its contract with the agency shows what the agency gets. Put both in one place and the margin is public.

This is the core insight. Construction has pay-app portals, but they work because the general contractor controls them. In IT chains, every tier guards its margin, so the information needs to flow without the contracts flowing.
