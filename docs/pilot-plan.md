# Pilot plan

Three steps, each with a clear exit test.

## Step 1: one prime, two subcontractors, on DevNet (4 to 6 weeks)

- One mid-size IT firm, one agency and one freelancer, each with their own party on Canton DevNet.
- Real milestones from one live project, entered as Sluice subcontracts. Payment still happens over the banks as today; Sluice records the acceptance, the obligation and the due date.
- **Integration:** CSV export of each firm's history into its own accounting.
- **Exit test:** every client acceptance reaches the freelancer within a day, and no firm sees another firm's contract.

## Step 2: payment proof from the bank (6 to 8 weeks)

- The prime's bank payment reference is attached to each obligation, so the receipt on Canton points to a real transfer.
- The prime's Form I list (dues over 45 days) is produced from Sluice for the half-year filing.
- **Integrations:** bank payment file or statement import; Form I report export.
- **Exit test:** the prime's Form I list from Sluice matches its books.

## Step 3: production on MainNet (after step 2)

- Move the pilot firms to Canton MainNet, connected to the Global Synchronizer.
- Invite the prime's other subcontractors, and the agency's other primes.
- Apply for Canton Featured App status.
- **Exit test:** the prime pays to continue, and at least one firm joins through an agency rather than through the prime.
